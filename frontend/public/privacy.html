Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Spring Command Center ("the App," "we," "us") is a private, internal business-intelligence application operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("the Company") for use by its authorized personnel. This policy explains what data the App accesses, and how that data is used, stored, and protected.

1. Who this applies to

The App is a private tool for the Company's own team. It is not offered to the public and does not create accounts for outside consumers. "Users" means the Company's authorized employees and contractors who are granted access.

2. Data we access

With authorization, the App connects to third-party systems the Company already uses and reads business data from them:

  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit): financial reporting data such as profit-and-loss summaries and account balances, for the Company's own entities.
  • Sisu: real estate production data (transactions, agents, pipeline).
  • Follow Up Boss: CRM data (leads, contacts, agent activity).
  • Arive: mortgage loan pipeline data.

We access QuickBooks data only through Intuit's official API using OAuth 2.0 authorization granted by an administrator of each QuickBooks company. We request access appropriate to financial reporting and read this data only to display the dashboards and metrics described below.

3. How we use the data

Data is used solely to provide internal financial and operational reporting to the Company's authorized Users: computing metrics, rendering dashboards, and flagging anomalies. We do not sell, rent, or share the data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

4. Personal information

Some connected data may include names or contact details of the Company's clients, agents, or borrowers. We handle this information only as needed to display the reporting described above, and we restrict access to authorized Users.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway.
  • All third-party access and refresh tokens, including QuickBooks tokens, are encrypted at rest.
  • API credentials (client IDs and secrets) are stored as secured environment variables and are never committed to source code.
  • Access requires authentication, and data is scoped to the Company's own tenant.
  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain connected data only as long as needed for reporting. An administrator may disconnect any integration at any time, which revokes the App's access. Upon disconnection or on request, the associated data is deleted from the App.

7. Disconnecting QuickBooks

An administrator can disconnect the App from any QuickBooks company at any time from the App's integration settings, or from within QuickBooks under connected apps. Disconnecting revokes the App's token through Intuit's revocation endpoint.

8. Third-party services

The App relies on third-party services (Intuit/QuickBooks, Sisu, Follow Up Boss, Arive, and Railway). Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest version.
